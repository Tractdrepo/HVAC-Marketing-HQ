RS Gonzales alternative for HVAC: an honest comparison

RS Gonzales vs HVAC Marketing HQ: published prices, a 12-month cost example with the math, contract and website terms, and where each fits.

By Logan, founder of HVAC Marketing HQ · Updated Oct 12, 2026

Key takeaways

  • RS Gonzales is a full-service agency that leads with HVAC and runs ads. Its plans are $2,200, $3,500 and $5,000 a month, plus ad spend, as published in Oct 2026.
  • Their pricing page lists a $3,000 a month minimum ad spend and a 12-month term. Their fine print says you own the website after 12 months or a $4,500 buyout.
  • They're a larger agency, about 28 to 30 people, built around paid ads.
  • We cost less, you own your site from day one, and ads are an add-on with no minimum spend.

The short version

RS Gonzales (rsgonzales.com) is an agency based in Washington, DC. It markets itself to HVAC companies, but its site also has pages for plumbers, electricians and other industries. It runs SEO, Google Ads, Local Services Ads, Meta and Yelp ads, and includes a website and a CRM with its plans. With a $3,000 a month minimum ad spend, it suits shops ready to spend on ads. We're a smaller, lower-cost option for owner-run shops that want a website they own, a Google profile that's run well, and fast follow-up on missed calls, without paid ads.

Everything about RS Gonzales on this page is as published on their site, Oct 2026. Prices and terms change, so check their pricing page and terms before you decide.

Contract and exit terms in their fine print

RS Gonzales puts its headline promises on almost every page: a new website, 0% markup on ad spend, no setup fees and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Their terms, refund policy and SEO packages page, as published in Oct 2026, add detail to each one:

  • "0% markup" applies up to your tier's ad spend cap. Spend above the cap carries a 10% fee.
  • "No setup fees" sits next to a $500 onboarding deposit that's non-refundable once work starts.
  • The 30-day money-back guarantee covers the first month only. If the new site is already live, you pay $4,500 or have your old site restored.
  • "You own your website" applies after 12 months or a $4,500 buyout.

Read the fine print before you sign, with any agency.

Where they're strong

  • Published case studies. Named client results on their site.
  • Large ad budgets. Google, Local Services, Meta and Yelp ads, for shops spending $3,000 a month or more.
  • Field software sync. Their CRM connects to ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge and Jobber.

Questions to ask on their sales call

  1. Is my plan 6 or 12 months? Their SEO packages page and pricing page differ for Grow. Get the term in writing.
  2. What's my tier's ad spend cap? Ask what you'd pay in the 10% fee if you go over it.
  3. Is the $500 deposit credited to my first invoice? And when exactly does it become non-refundable?
  4. If I leave in month six, what happens to my website? Ask whether the $4,500 buyout applies and what you'd get for it.
  5. What does my all-in monthly cost look like? Plan fee plus ad spend, added up, for your size of shop.
  6. Who will my account manager be? Ask how many clients they have today.
