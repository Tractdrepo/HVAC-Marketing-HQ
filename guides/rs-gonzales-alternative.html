RS Gonzales alternative for HVAC: an honest comparison

RS Gonzales and HVAC Marketing HQ side by side: published prices, a 12-month cost example with the math, contract and website terms, what they do better, and questions to ask on their sales call.

By Logan, founder of HVAC Marketing HQ · Updated Oct 12, 2026

Key takeaways

  • RS Gonzales is a full-service HVAC agency that runs ads. Its plans are $2,200, $3,500 and $5,000 a month, plus ad spend, as published in Oct 2026.
  • Their pricing page lists a $3,000 a month minimum ad spend and a 12-month term. Their fine print says you own the website after 12 months or a $4,500 buyout.
  • They have far more proof than we do: named case studies, a 5.0 Google rating and a team of about 28 to 30 people.
  • We cost less and run ads as an add-on with no minimum spend. If you want a big agency running a large ad budget, they may be the better fit.

The short version

RS Gonzales (rsgonzales.com) is an HVAC-only agency based in Washington, DC. It runs SEO, Google Ads, Local Services Ads, Meta and Yelp ads, and includes a website and a CRM with its plans. With a $3,000 a month minimum ad spend, it suits shops ready to spend on ads. We're a smaller, lower-cost option for owner-run shops that want a website they own, a Google profile that's run well, and fast follow-up on missed calls, without paid ads.

Everything about RS Gonzales on this page is as published on their site, Oct 2026. Prices and terms change, so check their pricing page and terms before you decide.

Contract and exit terms in their fine print

RS Gonzales puts its headline promises on almost every page: a new website, 0% markup on ad spend, no setup fees and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Their terms, refund policy and SEO packages page, as published in Oct 2026, add detail to each one:

  • "0% markup" applies up to your tier's ad spend cap. Spend above the cap carries a 10% fee.
  • "No setup fees" sits next to a $500 onboarding deposit that's non-refundable once work starts.
  • The 30-day money-back guarantee covers the first month only. If the new site is already live, you pay $4,500 or have your old site restored.
  • "You own your website" applies after 12 months or a $4,500 buyout.

None of this is unusual for a full-service agency, and they publish it, which is more than many do. Just read it before you sign.

What they do better

  • Real, named proof. 12 named case studies with dashboard screenshots, a 5.0 Google rating from about 40 reviews, and a reviews page that links each quote to Google. We don't have clients or case studies yet.
  • Paid ads, run for you. Google Ads, Local Services Ads, Meta and Yelp, with detailed cost-per-lead guidance on their channel pages. We run Google Ads, Local Services Ads and Facebook ads too, as add-ons.
  • A bigger team. About 28 to 30 staff by their count, with the owners and 11 staff named on their About page, and a stated cap of 12 clients per account manager.
  • A CRM included. Their CRM, included with every plan, covers missed-call text-back, booking, estimates and invoices, and syncs with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge and Jobber.
  • Content and openness. 92 blog posts, a day-by-day 90-day plan with a section on what day 90 does not guarantee, and a free founder-recorded video audit.

Questions to ask on their sales call

  1. Is my plan 6 or 12 months? Their SEO packages page and pricing page differ for Grow. Get the term in writing.
  2. What's my tier's ad spend cap? Ask what you'd pay in the 10% fee if you go over it.
  3. Is the $500 deposit credited to my first invoice? And when exactly does it become non-refundable?
  4. If I leave in month six, what happens to my website? Ask whether the $4,500 buyout applies and what you'd get for it.
  5. What does my all-in monthly cost look like? Plan fee plus ad spend, added up, for your size of shop.
  6. Who will my account manager be? Ask how many clients they have today.
