Footbridge Media alternative for HVAC: honest comparison

Footbridge Media vs HVAC Marketing HQ: published prices, a 12-month cost example with the math, guarantees, website terms and where each fits.

By Logan, founder of HVAC Marketing HQ · Updated Oct 12, 2026

Key takeaways

  • Footbridge Media costs much less than we do: $249 a month with no setup fee, as published in Oct 2026. That's $2,988 for 12 months, against $7,983 for our Starter plan.
  • It serves 15 trades, HVAC among them, and says it has worked with contractors since 2004.
  • Its Terms say you own the website once more than 90 days have passed, and on exit they zip up the files for you to download.
  • We cost more, work only with HVAC companies, and include missed-call text-back in every plan.

The short version

Footbridge Media (footbridgemedia.com) is a Pensacola, Florida agency that sells one flat plan to contractors in 15 trades. For $249 a month you get a website, hosting, SEO, Google profile management, citations, automated review requests, a CRM and a marketing consultant. It's hard to beat on price. Where we differ is HVAC focus, missed-call text-back, and how the website and guarantee terms read in the fine print.

Everything about Footbridge Media on this page is as published on their site, Oct 2026. Prices and terms change, so check their site and Terms before you decide.

Guarantees and website terms in their fine print

Footbridge's marketing pages make bold promises. Their Terms of Use (effective June 18, 2026), as published in Oct 2026, read a little differently:

  • Money-back. The 90-day guarantee is in the Terms. The Terms mention first contacting them to try to resolve any issues, then a refund "with no questions asked". Otherwise fees are non-refundable once work has started.
  • Price lock. Their "Three Year Fixed Price Pledge" is on their guarantees page but not in the Terms, which let them change terms without prior notice.
  • Website ownership. The Terms say you own the site once more than 90 days have passed since signup. On exit, they zip up the files and give you a download link. Their sites run on their own platform, not WordPress, and their FAQ adds that some subscription parts stop working after you cancel.
  • Ad accounts. Footbridge keeps ownership of the Google Ads account it creates for you. Ad accounts we set up are in your name.
  • Existing sites. They don't work on existing websites; every client gets a rebuild.

With us, you own your site, your domain, your Google profile and your reviews.

Where they're strong

  • Price. $249 a month with no setup fee, for shops that want the lowest monthly cost.
  • Longer money-back window. 90 days, against our first month back within 30 days of launch.
  • A CRM included. Booking, quotes, invoices and online payments come with the plan.

Questions to ask on their sales call

  1. If I leave, what exactly do I get? Ask whether the zip of files will run as a working site somewhere else, and what stops working.
  2. Is the three-year price pledge in my agreement? It's on their guarantees page but not in their Terms.
  3. Who owns the Google Ads account if I add ads? Their page says they keep it. Ask what you keep if you leave.
  4. How do I get a refund within 90 days? Ask what "try to resolve the issues first" involves.
  5. How are missed calls handled? Ask what happens when a homeowner calls and nobody picks up.
  6. How many HVAC clients does my consultant handle?

Questions

How much does Footbridge Media cost?

As published on their site in Oct 2026: $249 a month, with no setup or build fee and no contract. Nexstar and CertainPath members pay $194 a month. Ads and other add-ons cost extra.

Do you own your website with Footbridge Media?

Their Terms say you own the site once more than 90 days have passed since signup. On exit, they zip the files and give you a download link.

Is Footbridge Media HVAC only?

No. HVAC is one of 15 trades on their site.

Why does HVAC Marketing HQ cost more?

We include missed-call text-back in 10 seconds, the HQ Command Center dashboard, and a site you own, and we work only with HVAC companies. If price matters most, Footbridge is the cheaper option.
