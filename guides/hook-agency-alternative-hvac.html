Hook Agency alternative for HVAC: an honest comparison

Hook Agency vs HVAC Marketing HQ: published prices, a 12-month cost example with the math, contracts, ownership and where each one fits.

By Logan, founder of HVAC Marketing HQ · Updated Oct 12, 2026

Key takeaways

  • Hook Agency is a home-services agency for many trades, with HVAC one of them. Its forms list under $1M in revenue as not a fit, as published in Oct 2026.
  • Its published prices start at $2,800 a month for local SEO, and $12,000 or $24,000 for a website, paid over 12 months.
  • A website plus local SEO from Hook comes to $45,600 over 12 months. Our Starter plan comes to $7,983.
  • Hook is a larger, multi-trade agency aimed at shops over $1M. We're HVAC only and built for owner-run shops.

The short version

Hook Agency (hookagency.com) is a Minneapolis agency for home-service companies: roofing, HVAC, plumbing and more. It sells SEO, Google Maps SEO, AI search work, Google Ads, Local Services Ads, Meta ads and websites. It aims at shops over $1 million in revenue. We work only with HVAC companies, mostly owner-run shops, and run ads as an optional add-on.

Everything about Hook Agency on this page is as published on their site, Oct 2026. Their full Terms of Service sit in an embedded page we couldn't read, so check the contract itself before you sign.

Contracts and ownership

Hook is clear and generous on ownership. Its pricing page says "You own your website 100%" and that you own your ad account, with your own card paying for the spend. Its Meta ads page says you own your raw footage and ad creative.

On contract length, their pages say different things, as published in Oct 2026. The home page FAQ explains "a yearly commitment". Their main SEO page says SEO contracts start at 12 months, then go month to month. Their HVAC SEO page says "We have month to month contracts." Their website is paid over 12 months. Ask which applies to you, and get it in writing.

Where they're strong

  • Track record. Published case studies across several trades.
  • Ad creative. Photo and video shoots on their Meta ads plan.
  • Plain ownership terms. Website, ad accounts and creative are yours, in writing on their pricing page.

Questions to ask on their sales call

  1. Is my contract 12 months or month to month? Their pages say both. Get the term for each service in writing.
  2. If I cancel before month 12, what do I owe on the website? The website is paid over 12 months, so ask about the balance.
  3. Do I own the website from day one, or after the last payment?
  4. How many HVAC clients does my account team work with? Ask for HVAC case studies near your size.
  5. What's the all-in monthly cost? Services, website payment and ad spend, added up.
  6. What happens after the free audit or intro call? Ask what's in the proposal and how long it's valid.

Questions

How much does Hook Agency cost for HVAC?

As published on their site in Oct 2026, local SEO starts at $2,800 a month, Google Ads management from $2,000 a month, and websites cost $12,000 or $24,000 paid over 12 months. Bundles on their landing pages start at $3,500 a month.

Does Hook Agency work with small HVAC companies?

Their contact forms, as published in Oct 2026, list under $1M in revenue as not a fit at this time.

Do you own your website with Hook Agency?

Their pricing page says you own your website 100% and your ad account. Ask whether that applies from day one or after the 12 payments.

Is Hook Agency HVAC only?

No. HVAC is one of many trades it serves, alongside roofing, plumbing and others.
