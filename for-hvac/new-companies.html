Questions

Common questions

How long until a new HVAC company shows up on Google maps?

It depends on your town, your competition and how complete your profile is, so nobody can promise a date. What speeds it up is a verified profile with the right category, real reviews coming in every week and a site that backs up what the profile says.

Should a new HVAC company run Google ads right away?

Ads can bring calls fast, but they stop the day you stop paying. We'd get the profile, site, reviews and text-back working first, so any ad money lands on a shop that answers and looks trustworthy.

Can I ask friends and family for reviews?

Google's rules say reviews should come from real customer experiences. We only ask people you actually did work for, which keeps your profile safe.

I work out of my truck. Do I need an office address?

No. Google lets service businesses hide their address and set a service area instead. We set that up for you.
