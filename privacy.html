Privacy Policy

Draft for lawyer review before launch. Written to meet A2P 10DLC texting verification.

This policy explains what HVAC Marketing HQ ("we", "us") collects through this website and our services, and how we use it. Last updated: October 12, 2026.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you request a report card or contact us: your website, business name, the town you serve, first name, email address and phone number.
  • Public business information. To build your report card we look up public information about your business, such as your Google Business Profile, reviews, website and directory listings.
  • Usage information. Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited. If we add analytics tools such as Google Analytics, they may set cookies to measure how the site is used.
  • Chat and messages. If we add a web chat, we keep the messages you send so we can reply.

How we use it

  • To create and email your report card and answer your questions.
  • To send follow-up emails about your report card. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To text you, only if you checked the box agreeing to receive texts.
  • To protect the site from spam and abuse (for example with Cloudflare Turnstile).

Text messaging

If you opt in to text messages, we will text you about your report card and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text messaging opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers that deliver messages on our behalf.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run this site and our services, such as hosting (Vercel), email delivery (Resend), spam protection (Cloudflare), our CRM and messaging platform, and scheduling tools. They only use your information to provide services to us. We do not sell your personal information.

Client customer data

When we provide services to a business, we handle that business's customer data only on its instructions, never sell it, and notify the business of any breach as required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep report card requests and messages for as long as needed to serve you, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email, reply STOP to any text, or email hello@hvacmarketinghq.com to see, correct or delete your information.
