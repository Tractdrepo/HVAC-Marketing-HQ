Website redesign

A new site for your HVAC shop, without losing what already ranks.

Your site is slow, dated, or built by a cousin in 2016, but it has years of Google history. A redesign should fix the first part without throwing away the second. We move what works, redirect every old link and build the rest the right way.

Free report card in your inbox in about 5 minutes. Nobody calls you.

The problem

Where redesigns go wrong.

✕

Redesigns can wipe out rankings

A new site that changes every link without redirects can drop pages Google already trusted.

✕

Slow on a phone

Most HVAC searches happen on phones. A heavy theme with sliders and popups loses the caller before the page loads.

✕

No page for what you really sell

One 'Services' page can't rank for AC repair, heat pumps and installs in every town.

✕

You don't control it

Some sites live on an agency's platform. If you leave, the site stays with them.

Questions

Common questions

Will a redesign hurt my Google rankings?

It can if it's done carelessly. Keeping what works and redirecting every old link is how we avoid that. We can't promise rankings never move, but we don't throw away what you've built.

Can you redesign a site built on WordPress or Wix?

Yes. We move the content to our build and point your domain at it. Your domain stays registered to you.

What if my current agency owns the site?

We can rebuild from what's public and redirect the old links once your domain points to us. Your domain is the part that matters, and it should be in your name.

Do I need to write anything?

No. We write the pages from a short call and your answers to a few questions, and you approve before it goes live.
