Questions

Pricing questions

I got burned by an agency before. Why is this different?

Because you can check it. Prices are on this page, you own your site, domain and reviews, and you can open your dashboard anytime to see calls, texts and booked jobs. If it isn't working, you'll see it before we tell you.

How soon will I see more calls?

Missed-call text-back starts as soon as the phone carriers approve your texting registration. We file it at kickoff, and approval usually takes 1 to 3 weeks. Google rankings and reviews take longer: months, not weeks. That's why the first term is six months, and why your HQ Command Center shows what's moving along the way.

What do I have to do?

One kickoff call, sharing access to your domain, Google profile and phone line, approving your site before it goes live, and answering the texts. We do the rest.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, Google profile, reviews and site content are yours. If you leave, we hand over the site files and help move your number. It's written into the agreement.

How often do we meet?

Not often, on purpose. You're running jobs, not sitting in meetings. Every month you get a short recap and your statement to read when it suits you. We get on a strategy call twice a year on Growth and every quarter on Dominate and up. In between, text or email us anytime and we answer in the shared inbox.

How long is the contract?

Six months to start, then month to month. Six months gives the Google profile and reviews time to move. After that you stay because it's working. And if you're not happy in the first 30 days after launch, we refund your first month.

What if I already have a website I like?

Keep it. We can add the booking form and lead system to your current site, and the build fee is lower or waived.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. If you're not happy in your first 30 days after launch, tell us and we refund your first month. You keep your domain, Google profile and reviews either way.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You forward your existing line through the system, so customers keep calling the number they already know. We walk you through it during setup.

Does it work with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber?

Yes. Booking can go straight to your field-service software, or into our calendar if you don't use one. Review requests can trigger when a job is marked done.

Do I have to manage the software?

No. You and your office use one inbox app on your phones, and you can check your HQ Command Center dashboard anytime. We build and run everything behind it.

Do you run Google Ads or Local Services Ads?

Yes, as an add-on to any plan: Google Ads, Local Services Ads and Facebook ads. Your ad spend is billed by Google or Meta to your own account and never marked up, and calls from ads are tracked so you can see which ones turn into booked jobs.

How many Google reviews do I need?

There's no magic number. Steady, recent reviews beat a big pile of old ones, which is why every plan asks every finished job for one.

Can you get my shop recommended by ChatGPT?

We build what AI tools read: a complete Google profile, consistent listings, clear service pages and recent reviews. Nobody can honestly guarantee an AI answer, and we won't.

What if we can't handle more calls yet?

Tell us on the call. We'd rather wait, or start with text-back only, than send you work your crew can't get to.

Can I get missed-call text-back on its own?

Yes. Missed-call text-back is available on its own for $97 a month.

Who will I talk to?

A team that works only with HVAC companies. You get a direct line, not a ticket queue.

What counts as a page?

Page counts are the pages we write for you: your home page, service pages, about page and city pages. Starter is 5 pages in total, including 1 city page. Privacy, Terms, Accessibility and thank-you pages come with every site and don't count toward the total.

What's the build fee for?

Building your site, setting up your Google profile, forwarding your line through the text-back system and importing your past customers. Monthly billing starts when you go live, not when you sign.
