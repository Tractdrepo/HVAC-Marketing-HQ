Questions

Google & reviews questions

How long until I move up the map?

Profile fixes can help within weeks. Steady movement usually takes three to six months, longer in a big metro. We show you where you rank on your dashboard.

How many Google reviews do I need?

There's no magic number. Recent, steady reviews matter more than a big total.

What do you do about a bad review?

Reply calmly and professionally, offer to make it right, and keep good reviews coming so one bad one doesn't define you.

Do you run Google Ads or Local Services Ads?

Not yet. Ads work better once calls are answered and your profile is strong, so we start there. Ads are coming as an add-on.

Can you guarantee I'll be #1?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We guarantee the work: a complete profile, review requests after every job and a reply to every review.
