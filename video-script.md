# Founder intro video: script and how to record

The site shows a small "Hi, I'm Logan" bubble in the bottom-left corner. Tapping it plays this video. Aim for 60 to 75 seconds.

## How to record
- **Phone, held vertical.** The player is tall, like a Story.
- **Face at eye level and filling the top half of the frame.** A window in front of you, not behind you.
- **Somewhere quiet.** A truck, shop or office background is great if it looks like the trade.
- **Talk to one HVAC owner, not to "everyone".** Don't read word for word; hit the beats below.
- **Two or three takes, then send the best one.** I'll compress it, add captions and make the poster frame.

## Script (beats, say it your way)

**1. Hook (5 s)**
"If you run an HVAC company, this'll take a minute and might save you a lot of missed calls."

**2. Who you are (10 s)**
"I'm Logan. I started HVAC Marketing HQ because good HVAC shops lose jobs every day to shops that just answer faster and show up higher on Google."

**3. What we do (20 s)**
"We do three things. We answer every call you miss, with a text back in under a minute. We get you ranking on the map with a fast site and a Google profile that's actually run. And we turn every finished job into a review and the next job."

**4. Why trust us (15 s)**
"Our prices are on the site. You own your website and domain. If you're not happy in the first 30 days after launch, you get your first month back."

**5. Call to action (10 s)**
"Start with the free report card. Type in your website, and in about five minutes you'll see how you stack up against the shops near you. Nobody's going to call you."

## Don'ts
- No client results or numbers we can't prove yet.
- No "guaranteed #1 on Google."
- No prices until they're set.
