Tucson homes

The homes your techs walk into.

81% of homes in Tucson were built before 2000. Older homes mean replacements, duct work and repair calls. Most homes heat with electricity, so heat pumps and electric furnaces lead the winter work. 118,217 households own their home, and owners are the ones who buy new systems.

Tucson market

What Tucson's weather means for your marketing.

During the summer monsoon, humidity in Tucson runs much higher than in the rest of the year.

A cooling season most of the year

Tucson averages about 158 days a year at 90°F or hotter. AC repair and replacement pages lead your site, and every missed call in the summer surge gets a text back.

Mild winters, steady heat pump work

January lows average 40.8°F. Heat pump repair and installs carry the cooler months, with fewer emergency heating calls.

Dry air and dust

Dust works filters and outdoor units hard. Maintenance plan pages and filter reminders keep customers coming back between breakdowns.

A crowded map

With 542,629 people in the city alone, you won't win every search from one address. A page for each part of town you serve helps you show up beyond your shop's neighborhood.
