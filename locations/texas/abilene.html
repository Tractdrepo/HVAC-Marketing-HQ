Abilene market

What Abilene's weather means for your marketing.

Abilene's record high is 113 °F, and July averages a 96.4 °F high at Abilene Regional Airport.

Summers that break AC units

July highs average 96.4°F in Abilene. The hottest weeks bring the most calls and the most missed ones, so text-back and fast booking matter most then.

Mild winters, steady heat pump work

January lows average 33.7°F. Heat pump repair and installs carry the cooler months, with fewer emergency heating calls.

Humidity sells comfort

Humid air makes homes feel warmer than the thermostat says. Pages for dehumidifiers, indoor air quality and duct work bring higher-value jobs.

Reviews decide it

In a market this size, homeowners often pick between a handful of shops by star rating and how recent the reviews are. Steady review requests after every job are the fastest lever.
