Local Services Ads

Local Services Ads for HVAC, set up right and watched every week.

Local Services Ads sit at the very top of Google, with a badge and your star rating, and you pay for leads instead of clicks. They also need verification, the right service types, fast answers and someone to dispute the leads that weren't real. We handle all of it.

Free report card in your inbox in about 5 minutes. Nobody calls you.

The problem

Where Local Services Ads go wrong.

✕

Stuck in verification

Licenses, insurance and background checks have to match exactly, or the ads never go live.

✕

Paying for bad leads

Wrong service, wrong area or spam calls can be disputed, but only if someone checks every lead.

✕

Slow answers mean fewer leads

How fast and how often you answer affects how often your ads show. Missed calls cost twice.

✕

Reviews lag behind

Your star rating shows right on the ad. A thin review count makes the badge less convincing.

How it works

How we set it up.

You give us access and approve the work. We do the rest.

  1. 1

    We get you verified

    We walk you through the license, insurance and background checks Google asks for, and fix what's holding it up.

  2. 2

    We set services and areas

    Only the job types and towns you want, with a weekly budget you pick.

  3. 3

    Every lead is answered

    Missed-call text-back and the shared inbox catch the leads you can't pick up.

  4. 4

    We review every lead

    We dispute the ones that don't qualify and report booked jobs, not just lead counts.

Straight answer

Is it worth it for your shop?

Worth it if

  • You can pass Google's license, insurance and background checks
  • You answer most calls live
  • You'd rather pay per lead than per click

Skip it if

  • You can't get verified in your state
  • You can't answer leads quickly
Questions

Common questions

How do Local Services Ads charge?

You pay per lead, not per click. Google's help pages explain what counts as a lead and when you can ask for a credit. We check every lead for you.

Are Local Services Ads worth it for HVAC?

They can be, since the ad sits at the top with your rating. Run the numbers with our LSA cost calculator using your own cost per lead and close rate.

Do I still need my Google Business Profile?

Yes. Local Services Ads and the map are separate. Your profile and reviews keep working when your ad budget runs out.

Who owns the account?

You do. It's your business on the ad, and you keep it if you leave.
