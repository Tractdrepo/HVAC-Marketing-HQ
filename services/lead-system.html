Questions

Lead system questions

Does it replace ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber?

No. It sits in front of them and catches, answers and follows up on leads. Booked jobs can go straight into your field software.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. You forward your existing line through the system, so customers keep calling the number they know.

Is texting customers legal?

Yes, when it's done right. We register your texting with the carriers and every message carries opt-out instructions. That's part of setup.

Who sets it up?

We do. You get the app on your phone already set up, with your messages written and your team added.

What does it cost?

It's included in every plan. Normal texting and calling are included; large campaigns beyond your plan are billed at cost.
