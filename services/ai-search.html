AI search

Be the shop AI tools can read and trust.

More homeowners ask an AI assistant who to call. Those tools pull from your website, your Google profile, your reviews and your listings. We make sure what they read about you is clear, consistent and correct.

Free report card in your inbox in about 5 minutes. Nobody calls you.

The problem

Where the calls leak.

✕

Facts scattered or missing

If your hours, towns and services aren't stated plainly, AI tools guess or skip you.

✕

Listings that disagree

Different phone numbers or names across the web make you harder to trust.

✕

No structured data

Without schema markup, machines have to work out what your business is.

✕

Promises nobody can keep

No one controls what an AI tool recommends. Be wary of anyone who says they do.

Questions

Common questions

Can you get me recommended by ChatGPT?

Nobody can promise that. We make sure everything AI tools read about you is clear, correct and consistent, which is the part you can control.

Is this separate from SEO?

Mostly the same work: clear pages, structured data, good reviews and matching listings. We just make sure it's done with AI tools in mind too.
