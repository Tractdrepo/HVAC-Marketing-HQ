The problem

Why rented leads cost more than they look.

✕

Shared leads

Some lead sites sell the same homeowner to more than one shop. The fastest caller wins, and the rest still pay.

✕

You pay for the lead, not the job

Wrong numbers, price shoppers and people outside your area cost the same as real customers.

It stops when you stop paying

Rented leads build nothing. Turn them off and the phone goes quiet.

Leads that go cold

A lead nobody calls back quickly is usually gone, wherever it came from.

Questions

Common questions

Are your leads exclusive?

We don't sell leads. Calls and forms come from your own Google profile, website and ads, so they're yours alone.

Should I stop buying leads from lead sites?

Not on day one. Many shops keep a lead site running while their own channels grow, then cut back once the phone rings on its own. Your HQ Command Center shows where each booked job came from, so you decide with real numbers.

How fast will I get leads?

Ads can bring calls the week they go live. Google profile and SEO work builds over months. Most shops use both: ads for now, SEO for later.

What does HVAC lead generation cost with you?

Plans start at $499 a month. Ad management is a flat $299 a month per channel, and ad spend is billed to you by the platform, never marked up.
