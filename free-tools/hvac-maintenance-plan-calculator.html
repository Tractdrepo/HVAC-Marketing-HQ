The math

How it's worked out.

No hidden numbers. Here's every step.

  • Plan revenue = members x plan price a year
  • Repair revenue = members x extra repair revenue per member
  • Total this year = plan revenue + repair revenue
  • Members next year = members x renewal rate
Improve the number

Three ways to move it.

Offer the plan on every call

Every service visit is a chance to sign a member.

Remind before renewal

A text before the plan runs out keeps more members.

Book tune-ups early

Seasonal reminders fill slow weeks with member visits.
