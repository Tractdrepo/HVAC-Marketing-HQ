Questions

Website questions

How long does a new site take?

14 days from when we get your photos and details. Most of that time is us writing and building; your part is one kickoff call and one review.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and the site's content is yours. If you leave, we hand over the files.

Can I edit it myself?

You can, but you don't have to. Send us changes by text or email and they're included in your plan.

Is it WordPress?

No. Your site is custom-coded and served from a global network, so there are no plugins to update and nothing to hack. That's why it loads so fast.

I already have a site I like. Do I need a new one?

No. We can add the booking form and lead system to your current site, and the build fee is lower or waived.

Who writes the pages?

We do, from your kickoff call. You review everything before it goes live.

How is this different from Wix or a freelancer?

A builder gives you a template and leaves the rest to you. A freelancer usually builds and leaves. We build it, keep it updated, connect it to text-back and reviews, and show you the results.
