Google Business Profile

A Google profile that's actually run, not just claimed.

For most homeowners, your Google profile is the first thing they see, before your website. Stars, hours, photos and a call button in the map pack. We set it up right and keep it active.

Free report card in your inbox in about 5 minutes. Nobody calls you.

The problem

Where the calls leak.

✕

Wrong or missing categories

The main category is one of the biggest things Google uses to decide who shows up. Many shops have the wrong one.

✕

Services not listed

If AC repair, heat pumps and duct work aren't on your profile, Google has less reason to show you.

✕

A profile that looks abandoned

No recent photos, posts or review replies makes you look closed next to an active competitor.

✕

Fake listings around you

Spam listings with keyword-stuffed names can push real shops down.

Questions

Common questions

Can you guarantee the top 3 on the map?

No one honest can. Distance from the searcher is a big part of it, and nobody controls that. We fix what Google says matters and show you where you rank every month.

Who owns the profile?

You do. It stays in your name, and we're added as a manager.

Do I need a storefront?

No. Shops that work out of trucks can hide their address and set a service area. We set that up.
